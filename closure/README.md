# closure/

Stores the final records made when work is completed and formally closed.

## What belongs here

- Closure records confirming that work has been completed
- Completion summaries describing what was delivered
- Final decisions taken at the point of closure
- Lessons learned, so future work benefits from them
- Closure evidence supporting the sign-off

## What does not belong here

- Work still in progress — closure records are written once work is finished
- Transfer material for ongoing work — that belongs in `handover/`
- The full body of supporting proof — that belongs in `evidence/`; reference it
  from here rather than duplicating it

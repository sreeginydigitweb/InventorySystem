# handover/

Stores everything required to transfer work to another person or team.

## What belongs here

- Handover notes describing what has been done and what remains
- Current status of the work at the point of transfer
- Prerequisites the receiving person or team needs — access, tools, context
- Next actions, clearly stated so the work can be picked up without guesswork
- Known issues, open questions, and anything in progress

A handover should be complete enough that someone unfamiliar with the work can
continue it.

## What does not belong here

- Final completion records and closure sign-off — those belong in `closure/`
- General project documentation not specific to a transfer — that belongs in
  `documentation/`
- Credentials or access secrets; reference how access is granted, never the
  secret itself
